# Feedback on Business Requirements

You have many good business rules, but:

- For some of your rules, you are listing individual database constraints instead of business rules. Business rules are a set of contraints in the database that satisfy the needs of the final user.
- Some items are application logic (notifications, offensive word filtering, AI behavior). The database won't be able to handle these. 

Example of a good business rule  (based on your database topic):

Business Rule: Incident and Maintenance Management

Purpose:  
Ensure incidents and maintenance activities are tracked consistently and resolved properly.

Description:  
Duplicate incidents must be avoided, severity should reflect multiple reports, and maintenance tickets must be created and completed before incidents are resolved. Incidents cannot be closed without proper updates, and system status (trip/route) must reflect active issues.

Assumptions:  
Incidents, tickets, and routes are linked. Severity levels and statuses are predefined.


## What to fix:
- Group database contraints into business rules
- Remove or rethink application-level logic
- Focus on what the database enforces
